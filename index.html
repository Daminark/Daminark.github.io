About

I am a mathematician working in quantum error correction, with an emphasis on decoders. Some of my projects in this area are described on the projects page, and the code is on my GitHub.

I finished my PhD in Mathematics at the University of Wisconsin-Madison, advised by Simon Marshall. My dissertation work concerned the cutoff phenomenon for random walks on high-dimensional expanders.

My mathematical interests are broad, running through analysis on groups, graphs, probability and dynamics, and I enjoy explaining ideas across fields; some of that writing is here.

Previously, I was an undergraduate student at the University of Chicago.
