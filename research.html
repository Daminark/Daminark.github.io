Research

Random Walks on Density-Ramanujan Complexes

PhD dissertation, University of Wisconsin-Madison, 2026. Advisor: Simon Marshall. A paper based on this work is in preparation.

Download the dissertation (PDF, 45 pages)

Abstract

Let $\mathbf{G}$ be a semisimple group over a non-archimedean local field $F$, and let $\mathcal{B}$ be the Bruhat-Tits building of $G = \mathbf{G}(F)$. In [LLP20], it was shown that if $T$ is a $G$-equivariant, regular, and collision-free branching operator on a set of cells $\mathfrak{C} \subset \mathcal{B}$, then the induced walks on Ramanujan quotients $\Gamma \backslash \mathcal{B}$ exhibit the cutoff phenomenon. For $\mathbf{G} = \mathrm{PGL}_d$, simple random walks on Ramanujan complexes were also shown to exhibit cutoff in [CP22]. In this dissertation, we establish cutoff for these walks under the weaker assumption that the complexes form an expander family and satisfy Sarnak's density hypothesis.

  • [CP22] M. Chapman and O. Parzanchevski, Cutoff on Ramanujan complexes and classical groups, Comment. Math. Helv. 97 (2022), no. 3, 431-456.
